Trips

Explore some of our past trips

Experience the heart-pounding excitement of the Colorado River as it winds through the breathtaking Grand Canyon. This multi-day trip combines thrilling Class III and IV rapids with stunning vistas of ancient rock formations. Each day begins with a hearty breakfast before navigating famous rapids like Lava Falls and Hermit Rapids, offering a mix of adrenaline and camaraderie. Evenings are spent under a starlit sky, with campfires and stories shared among fellow adventurers. This is perfect for those seeking both adventure and awe-inspiring natural beauty.

Join a family-friendly rafting trip on the New River, ideal for all ages. This scenic journey features gentle Class I and II rapids, perfect for beginners and kids. As you paddle through lush forests and stunning cliffs, expert guides provide insight into the area’s rich history and diverse wildlife. Stop for a riverside picnic, take a dip in calm pools, or try your hand at fishing. With plenty of opportunities for laughter and bonding, this trip is a memorable way for families to connect with nature and each other.

Escape into the wild with a peaceful rafting trip on the Salmon River. This guided journey combines stunning landscapes with relaxing Class II and III rapids, perfect for those looking to unwind. Glide past towering cliffs and lush forests while keeping an eye out for local wildlife, including deer and eagles. Each evening is spent camping along the riverbank, where you can enjoy campfire meals and share stories under the vast Idaho sky. Ideal for those seeking a tranquil adventure, this trip offers a perfect blend of relaxation and exploration.
